# Product: Jiang Dong — Elite AI Architect Portfolio

## Register
brand

## Users
Headhunters, hiring managers, and technical leaders at top-tier financial institutions and AI companies in China and globally. They are pressed for time, visually discerning, and expect a portfolio that reflects the candidate's claimed caliber — "Elite AI Architect."

## Product Purpose
A bilingual (Chinese/English) personal portfolio website that presents Jiang Dong as a senior AI Technical Expert and FinTech leader. The website acts as a living, interactive resume — it must feel premium, technically credible, and distinct from generic portfolio templates. The design IS the first impression.

## Brand Tone
Confident without being loud. Technically grounded. Premium. The visual language should suggest deep expertise — not an agency portfolio, not a startup landing page, but the personal page of someone who builds AI infrastructure for major financial institutions.

## Anti-references
- No SaaS-cream light minimalism
- No neon-on-black "hacker" aesthetics
- No generic blue gradient hero sections
- No particle backgrounds that feel like generic templates (must feel purposeful and intelligent)
- No identical card grids

## Strategic Principles
- Every design decision should reinforce the "Senior AI Expert" positioning
- Bilingual parity: Chinese and English must feel equally polished
- Motion must feel intelligent and intentional — not decorative
- The chatbot is a statement: this person can deploy AI
